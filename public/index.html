Security & Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Your Data. Welcome to the MiniSpector Logs Portal. This portal is designed to help you manage ROV operational data securely. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users and the confidentiality of the operational logs stored here.

1. What Data Do We Collect?

We collect only the data needed to run this logging system:

  • Your User Profile: We collect your professional contact info (name, work email, company) to create your account and secure your access.
  • The Operational Logs: The main purpose of the portal is to store the dive details, equipment status, and maintenance reports you enter. (For this data, your employer is the "Data Controller," and we are the "Data Processor.")
  • Technical Usage Info: We automatically collect basic data from your web browser (like your IP address) to keep the system secure and running smoothly.

2. How Do We Use Your Data?

We use this data for the following essential functions:

  • To let you log in and access your specific project logs.
  • To enable the "automatic save" feature and keep reports synced in real-time.
  • To provide you with technical support when you need it.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Secure Servers: We store your data on secure servers.
  • Encryption: We use advanced encryption to protect your data while it travels over the Internet and while it is stored in our database.
  • Restricted Access: Access to logs is strictly limited to authorized users associated with that specific operation.

4. Data Retention

We keep your operational logs according to the agreement we have with your company. User profile data is kept as long as your account is active.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to review and correct your user profile data. For questions about the operational data in the logs themselves, please contact your employer.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about data security or privacy, please contact us at: sd@mcsoil.com
